## Glance at Backup Plans
The headline on [Backup Plans](app:overview) says whether everything is kept. A plan that needs attention shows as a red or amber card with the reason and **Try again**.

## Use the menu bar
Keepr's menu bar icon shows a dot while it backs up and a mark when a plan needs attention. Click it for each plan's last and next backup, without opening the window. Open the window with **Open Keepr**, or [Backup Plans](app:overview) from here.

## Choose what you're told
In [Settings](app:settings), Keepr tells you when a backup fails, and warns when a plan hasn't backed up for a few days. Turn on **Tell me when a backup finishes** too if you like.

## Read the log
When something went wrong, [Activity](app:activity) has every backup's result. Click one for its log, which says what was backed up, skipped or couldn't be read.
