Closing the window doesn't quit Keepr: it leaves the Dock and keeps backing up from the menu bar.

## The icon
Keepr's icon in the menu bar shows a dot while it's backing up, and a mark when a plan needs attention.

## The panel
Click the icon for whether everything is kept, the backup that's running with its time left, and each plan's last and next backup or its problem. Click a plan to open Keepr.

## Its menu
**Back up all now** (`⌘B`), **Pause backups for an hour** (which also stops the backup that's running), **Restore a file…** (`⌘R`), **Open Keepr** (`⌘O`), **Settings…** (`⌘,`) and **Quit Keepr** (`⌘Q`). Quit Keepr is the only way to stop it backing up.

## Open at Login
The switch above Open Keepr is the same as [Open at Login](app:settings) in Settings: it starts Keepr in the menu bar when you log in. It's only there in the installed app.
