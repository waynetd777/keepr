## Find it
If you know its name, type it in the toolbar's search box (`⌘K`) and press Return to search every backup; click a result to open it in Restore. Otherwise open [Restore](app:restore) and pick its plan.

## Go back to when it was right
Step through the snapshots with **Earlier** and **Later** (or `←` and `→`) in [Restore](app:restore), or click a bar in the strip. Turn on **Show deleted files** to see files that have gone since.

## Check the version
Click the file in [Restore](app:restore) to see every version kept. **Quick Look** shows one without restoring it, and **Compare with current** shows how it differs from the file on the Mac now.

## Put it back
Tick it, choose **Restore to** and what to do **If a file is already there**, and click **Restore**. **Keep both** leaves the file on the Mac as it is and adds the restored copy beside it. The restore shows in [Activity](app:activity).
