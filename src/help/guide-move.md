## Install Keepr and reach the backup
On the new computer, install Keepr and connect the drive, share, cloud folder or bucket the old one backed up to. If it isn't a destination here yet, [add it](app:destinations/add). A backup made on a Mac opens on a PC, and the other way round.

## Open the backup
On [Backup Plans](app:overview), click **Open backup…** (or **Open a backup…** on a new Keepr's welcome), pick the destination and the backup in it, and enter its password or recovery key if it's encrypted. It opens as a plan that's turned off.

## Restore your files
In [Restore](app:restore), pick the opened plan, tick the folders you want, choose **Another folder…** under **Restore to** if the old folders don't exist here, and click **Restore**. [Activity](app:activity) shows it as it goes.

## Carry on backing up
To keep backing up into the same backup, turn the plan on with **Plan on**, from [Backup Plans](app:overview) with **Edit…**, and check its folders are the ones on this computer. Or [make a new plan](app:plans/new) and keep the old backup as it is.
